## Use Schema on your Mac

1. Follow the download and setup instructions in your invitation.
2. Open Schema and finish the welcome steps.
3. Press **Option + Space** to open the bar, or use the shortcut you chose during setup.
4. Type “What is the weather in Boston?” and press **Return**.
5. Read the answer in the bar.

Continue with [getting started on Mac](/mac/getting-started) for voice, accounts, and your first conversation.
