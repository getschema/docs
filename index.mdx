Our goal is to build the Cloudflare of **web agents**, with APIs for convoluted services available for agents. **Computer Use isn't the future of machine→machine interaction.** We've wasted all this time and money on training models how to use a human interface, instead of focusing on the actual integration of machine→machine communication. The way that machines interact should be fundamentally different from the way humans and machines interact; and our goal is to be the translator between them.
