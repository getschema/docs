## Get the app

[Join the waitlist](https://form.typeform.com/to/exWUquA1). When you receive an invitation, use its download and setup instructions. If asked to sign in, use the email address you joined with.

## Finish the welcome steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your shortcut">
    Open Schema and select **Get started**. Keep **Option + Space**, or click the shortcut and press the keys you prefer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose voice and location preferences">
    Voice and location are optional. Enable voice if you want to speak your requests. Enable location if you want requests such as “What's the weather?” to use your current area. You can also name a city in your request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect accounts when you need them">
    Follow **Sign in to Google** if you want to connect Google, or continue and add accounts later. You can make your first public web request without adding an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Schema">
    Finish setup and select **Open Schema**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make your first request

1. Open the bar with your shortcut.
2. Type “What is the weather in Boston?”
3. Press **Return**.
4. Read the answer, then try “What about tomorrow?”

Press **Command + N** to start a new conversation when you change topics.

## Change your preferences

Type **/settings** in the bar and press **Return**. In **General**, you can change your shortcut, voice preferences, and location.

Continue with [using Schema](/mac/using-schema) or [accounts and sign-in](/mac/accounts).
