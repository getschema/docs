---
title: "Accounts and sign-in"
description: "Connect a website, manage saved accounts, and answer sign-in prompts."
---

Some requests need an account on the website you want to use. You can sign in to a site or add a saved account from Schema's settings.

## Sign in to a website

1. Type **/settings** in the bar and press **Return**.
2. Select **Signed in**.
3. Enter the website under **Sign in to a site**.
4. Select **Sign in**.
5. Complete sign-in in the window that opens, then close that window.

Use the website's normal sign-in steps, including any verification it asks you to complete.

## Add a saved account

1. Type **/accounts** and press **Return**.
2. Select **Add account**.
3. Enter the **Website** and a recognizable **Name**, such as “GitHub (work).”
4. Fill in the login details the account needs. Use **Add a field** for additional details.
5. Select **Save**.

Use the account form for passwords and other sign-in details.

## Update or remove an account

Open **/accounts** and select the account you want to change. Update its details and select **Save**, or select **Delete** to remove it. Fields left empty while editing keep their saved values.

## Answer a sign-in prompt

If Schema needs an extra detail during a task, such as a code sent by text, it asks in the bar. Enter the requested detail in the private input and press **Return**.

## Sign out

Open **Settings → Signed in** and use **Sign out everywhere** to sign out of connected sites. Saved accounts remain available.

To remove all saved accounts, open **Settings → Privacy** and select **Delete all** under **Saved accounts**.
