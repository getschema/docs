Schema for Mac is an assistant you can ask for help with everyday tasks on the web. Open the bar, type or say what you need, and read the result without leaving what you were doing.

## What you can ask

- Find and compare flights.
- Look up facts or check the weather.
- Find a website or page.
- Get help with a task that uses one of your accounts.

Be specific about dates, places, and preferences. For example:

> Find a round-trip flight from Seattle to Chicago, November 3 to 10, on Google Flights.

## Your first conversation

Press **Option + Space**, type a request, and press **Return**. You can ask follow-up questions in the same conversation.

When Schema presents options, choose the one you want. Use **Open page ↗** to continue on the website, including finishing a booking.
